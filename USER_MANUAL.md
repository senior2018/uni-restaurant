## Getting Started

### System Overview
**Our Restaurant** is a comprehensive web-based restaurant management system designed for university environments. The system allows users to browse menus, place orders, manage restaurant operations, and provide feedback.

### Accessing the System
- **URL**: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com
- **Browser Requirements**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions)
- **Mobile Support**: Fully responsive design for mobile devices

### User Roles
The system supports three main user roles:
- **Customer**: Students, faculty, and staff who place orders
- **Staff**: Restaurant employees who manage orders and operations
- **Admin**: Restaurant managers with full system access

## Customer Guide

### 1. Account Creation & Login

#### 1.1 Creating an Account
1. **Navigate to Registration**:
   - Click "Register" on the homepage
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/register

2. **Fill Registration Form**:
   - **Name**: Your full name
   - **Email**: Your university email address
   - **Phone**: Your contact number (required)
   - **Location**: Your permanent location (required)
   - **Password**: Strong password (8+ characters with mixed case, numbers, symbols)

3. **Email Verification**:
   - Check your email for a 6-digit verification code
   - Enter the code on the verification page
   - Code expires in 10 minutes

#### 1.2 Google Login
1. **Click "Continue with Google"** on the login page
2. **Grant Permissions** in the Google popup
3. **Complete Profile** (if first time):
   - Add your phone number
   - Add your permanent location
   - Set a password for future logins

#### 1.3 Login Process
1. **Navigate to Login**:
   - Click "Login" on the homepage
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/login

2. **Enter Credentials**:
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Click "Login"

3. **Account Lockout**:
   - After 3 failed attempts, your account will be locked
   - Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password

### 2. Browsing the Menu

#### 2.1 Viewing Available Meals
1. **Access Menu**:
   - Click "Menu" in the navigation
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/menu

2. **Menu Features**:
   - **Categories**: Browse by meal categories (Appetizers, Main Courses, etc.)
   - **Meal Details**: View descriptions, prices, and images
   - **Availability**: Only available meals are shown
   - **Search**: Use browser search (Ctrl+F) to find specific items

#### 2.2 Menu Information
- **Price Display**: All prices shown in local currency
- **Availability**: Real-time availability updates
- **Images**: High-quality meal images
- **Descriptions**: Detailed meal descriptions

### 3. Placing Orders

#### 3.1 Adding Items to Cart
1. **Select Meal**:
   - Click on any available meal
   - View meal details and price

2. **Add to Cart**:
   - Click "Add to Cart" button
   - Item is added to your cart
   - Cart icon shows item count

3. **Cart Management**:
   - Click "My Plate" to view cart
   - Adjust quantities using +/- buttons
   - Remove items using trash icon

#### 3.2 Checkout Process
1. **Review Cart**:
   - Click "My Plate" to view all items
   - Verify quantities and prices
   - Total price is calculated automatically

2. **Proceed to Checkout**:
   - Click "Proceed to Checkout"
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/checkout

3. **Checkout Information**:
   - **Delivery Location**: Confirm your delivery location
   - **Payment Method**: Select payment method (Cash, Mobile Money, Card)
   - **Order Notes**: Add any special instructions

4. **Place Order**:
   - Review all information
   - Click "Place Order"
   - You'll receive an email confirmation

### 4. Order Management

#### 4.1 Viewing Order History
1. **Access My Orders**:
   - Click "My Orders" in navigation
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/my-orders

2. **Order Information**:
   - **Order Number**: Unique order identifier
   - **Status**: Current order status
   - **Items**: List of ordered items
   - **Total Price**: Order total
   - **Date/Time**: Order placement time

#### 4.2 Order Statuses
- **Pending**: Order received, awaiting confirmation
- **Confirmed**: Order confirmed by restaurant
- **Preparing**: Order being prepared
- **Ready**: Order ready for pickup/delivery
- **Delivered**: Order completed
- **Cancelled**: Order cancelled

#### 4.3 Order Cancellation
1. **Cancel Order**:
   - Go to "My Orders"
   - Find the order you want to cancel
   - Click "Cancel Order"
   - Provide cancellation reason

2. **Cancellation Process**:
   - Cancellation request is sent to admin
   - Admin will approve or deny the request
   - You'll receive email notification of the decision

### 5. Rating & Reviews

#### 5.1 Rating Orders
1. **Access Rating**:
   - Go to "My Orders"
   - Find a delivered order
   - Click "Rate Order"

2. **Rating Process**:
   - **Star Rating**: Select 1-5 stars
   - **Comment**: Add optional comments
   - **Submit Rating**: Click "Submit Rating"

3. **Rating Guidelines**:
   - Rate based on food quality
   - Consider delivery service
   - Provide constructive feedback
   - Be respectful in comments

### 6. Support & Issues

#### 6.1 Creating Alerts
1. **Report Issues**:
   - Go to "My Orders"
   - Find the problematic order
   - Click "Report Issue"

2. **Alert Types**:
   - **Food Quality**: Issues with meal quality
   - **Delivery Issues**: Problems with delivery
   - **Service Problems**: Staff-related issues

3. **Alert Process**:
   - Provide detailed description
   - Select priority level
   - Submit alert
   - Staff will respond and resolve

#### 6.2 Support Tickets
1. **Contact Support**:
   - Click "Contact Support" in navigation
   - Or visit: https://the-restaurant-b5fz.onrender.com/contact

2. **Ticket Information**:
   - **Name**: Your full name
   - **Email**: Your contact email
   - **Subject**: Brief description of issue
   - **Message**: Detailed description

3. **Ticket Process**:
   - Submit ticket
   - Receive email confirmation
   - Admin will respond via email
   - Track ticket status

### 7. Profile Management

#### 7.1 Updating Profile
1. **Access Profile**:
   - Click your name in the top navigation
   - Select "Profile"

2. **Editable Information**:
   - **Name**: Update your display name
   - **Phone**: Change contact number
   - **Location**: Update permanent location
   - **Password**: Change your password

3. **Save Changes**:
   - Make desired changes
   - Click "Save Changes"
   - Changes are applied immediately

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### 1. Login Problems
**Issue**: Cannot login to the system
**Solutions**:
- Check email and password spelling
- Ensure caps lock is off
- Try password reset if account is locked
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try different browser

#### 2. Email Verification Issues
**Issue**: Not receiving verification emails
**Solutions**:
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Wait a few minutes for email delivery
- Try resending verification code
- Contact support if issue persists

#### 3. Order Placement Problems
**Issue**: Cannot place orders
**Solutions**:
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check that meals are available
- Verify delivery location is set
- Clear browser cache
- Try refreshing the page

#### 4. Payment Issues
**Issue**: Payment method not working
**Solutions**:
- Verify payment method details
- Check with your bank/card provider
- Try different payment method
- Contact support for assistance

#### 5. Mobile Access Issues
**Issue**: System not working on mobile
**Solutions**:
- Update mobile browser
- Clear browser cache
- Check internet connection
- Try different mobile browser
- Use desktop version if needed

### Error Messages & Solutions

#### 1. "Account Locked" Error
**Cause**: Too many failed login attempts
**Solution**: Use "Forgot Password" to reset password

#### 2. "Email Not Verified" Error
**Cause**: Email verification not completed
**Solution**: Check email for verification code and enter it

#### 3. "Profile Incomplete" Error
**Cause**: Required profile information missing
**Solution**: Complete profile with phone and location

#### 4. "Order Not Found" Error
**Cause**: Order ID is invalid or order was deleted
**Solution**: Check order number or contact support

#### 5. "Meal Not Available" Error
**Cause**: Meal is out of stock or unavailable
**Solution**: Choose different meal or wait for availability

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

#### Q: How do I create an account?
A: Click "Register" on the homepage, fill in your details, and verify your email with the 6-digit code sent to you.

#### Q: Can I use Google to login?
A: Yes, click "Continue with Google" on the login page. You'll need to complete your profile after first login.

#### Q: What if I forget my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions to reset your password.

#### Q: Is the system mobile-friendly?
A: Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on all mobile devices.

### Order Questions

#### Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse the menu, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, and place your order.

#### Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, you can request cancellation through "My Orders". Admin approval is required.

#### Q: How do I track my order?
A: Go to "My Orders" to see your order status and track progress.

#### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Cash, Mobile Money, and Card payments are supported.

### Account Questions

#### Q: How do I update my profile?
A: Click your name in the top navigation and select "Profile" to update your information.

#### Q: Can I change my email address?
A: Contact support to change your email address as it's used for account verification.

#### Q: What if my account is locked?
A: Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password and unlock your account.

### Support Questions

#### Q: How do I report an issue?
A: Use the "Report Issue" feature in "My Orders" or submit a support ticket through "Contact Support".

#### Q: How long does it take to get a response?
A: Support tickets are typically responded to within 24 hours during business hours.

#### Q: Can I rate my orders?
A: Yes, you can rate delivered orders through "My Orders" with a 1-5 star rating and optional comments.

### Technical Questions

#### Q: What browsers are supported?
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions) are supported.

#### Q: Do I need to install any software?
A: No, the system is web-based and works through your browser.

#### Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, the system uses industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
